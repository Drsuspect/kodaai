# KODAAI

## Voice-First AI for Accessible Education, Industrial Voice Command, and Turkish Language Technologies

[Türkçe](README.tr.md) · [Project Vision](VISION.md) · [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) · [Accessibility](ACCESSIBILITY.md) · [Website](https://kodaai.com.tr)

KODAAI is an independent **voice-first artificial intelligence and accessible technology initiative** building systems that let people interact with digital services, learning environments, and operational workflows through natural voice.

Our first product, **KODAAI Education**, is a voice-first learning platform designed with a **100% accessibility target** for blind and visually impaired learners preparing for Türkiye’s EKPSS examination.

KODAAI also conducts parallel research and development in:

- industrial voice command and control;
- Turkish large language models;
- Turkish text-to-speech technology;
- accessible and assistive AI;
- applied artificial intelligence for education and industry.

> This is KODAAI’s public project and collaboration repository. Proprietary source code, private datasets, production infrastructure, user data, voice recordings, and unpublished research are not included.

## Why Voice-First?

Most digital products are designed visually and receive voice support later. KODAAI starts from a different premise: voice is the primary interaction layer.

This approach is intended to support:

- independent access for blind and visually impaired users;
- low-friction interaction in hands-busy or eyes-busy environments;
- faster access to operational knowledge;
- Turkish-first language and speech experiences;
- resilient local and offline-capable workflows where appropriate.

## First Step: KODAAI Education

KODAAI Education enables learners to navigate lessons, answer topic quizzes, complete timed mock examinations, hear results, and control playback using voice.

Current focus areas include:

- EKPSS preparation for blind and visually impaired candidates;
- voice-controlled reading, quiz, and exam flows;
- deterministic local audio assets;
- web and Android experiences;
- keyboard and screen-reader compatibility;
- user-tested accessibility improvements;
- privacy-aware progress and session telemetry.

## Accessibility Principles

1. Voice is a primary interface, not an optional add-on.
2. Critical actions must not depend on visual feedback alone.
3. Keyboard and screen-reader paths remain available.
4. Ambiguous commands require safe recovery or confirmation.
5. Accessibility decisions are validated with target users.
6. Personal data and voice recordings follow data-minimization principles.
