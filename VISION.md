# KODAAI Vision

KODAAI is building a Turkish-first voice technology ecosystem where natural speech becomes a reliable primary interface for learning, digital access, and industrial operations.

## Mission

Develop human-centered voice-first AI systems that are accessible, secure, locally relevant, and capable of operating across education and industry.

## Strategic pillars

### 1. Accessibility by design

Accessibility is a starting condition, not a compliance patch. KODAAI Education is the first product built around this principle, with blind and visually impaired users at the center of the interaction model.

### 2. Turkish language intelligence

KODAAI researches Turkish command understanding, dialogue, domain terminology, pronunciation, long-form listening comfort, and speech synthesis.

### 3. Industrial voice interaction

The Industrial Voice Command Center explores secure voice access to information and appropriate workflows in industrial organizations. Authorization, confirmation, auditability, offline resilience, and safe failure behavior are core requirements.

### 4. Local-first resilience

Where appropriate, KODAAI favors deterministic local assets and offline-capable components to reduce latency, dependency, and operational risk.

### 5. Responsible applied AI

AI capabilities must be measurable, privacy-aware, explainable at the interaction level, and tested against real user needs.

## Current portfolio

- **KODAAI Education:** private beta and active product development.
- **Industrial Voice Command Center:** unpublished research and prototyping.
- **Turkish LLM:** research and domain experimentation.
- **Turkish TTS Engine:** research into natural and accessible Turkish synthesis.
- **Applied AI:** exploratory work across accessibility, education, and industry.

This vision describes active research directions, not completed-product claims or delivery-date commitments.
