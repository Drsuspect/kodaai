# KODAAI Roadmap

This roadmap communicates direction rather than fixed delivery dates.

## Now

- Validate KODAAI Education with blind and visually impaired beta users.
- Strengthen voice authentication, reading, quiz, and exam flows.
- Improve offline-friendly speech controls and playback reliability.
- Expand EKPSS learning and assessment content.
- Mature telemetry, privacy controls, deployment, and operational tooling.

## Next

- Publish structured accessibility evidence and testing methodology.
- Develop institutional collaboration models for accessible education.
- Prototype secure industrial voice-command workflows.
- Advance Turkish command-understanding and domain-language research.
- Evaluate Turkish TTS quality for education and long-form listening.

## Later

- Establish reusable KODAAI Voice Core components across product lines.
- Develop organization-specific voice interfaces with auditable authorization.
- Explore Turkish LLM specialization and evaluation datasets.
- Build a Turkish TTS engine focused on naturalness, intelligibility, and accessibility.
- Form research, education, and industrial partnerships.

## Release policy

Public announcements will distinguish clearly between research, prototype, beta, and production-ready capabilities. Private source code, user data, operational infrastructure, and unpublished research will remain protected.
